Private tool for one person

Personal automation, under Robert’s direction.

Hermes is a private, single-user automation service run by Robert Piazza on his private Hermes host. It helps carry out the tasks he asks it to perform.

Connected work, on request

When Robert chooses to connect Google services, Hermes can help with user-directed work across Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, Google Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets. Examples include finding information, preparing or sending email, reviewing calendar details, and working with files or documents.

Purpose and boundaries

These integrations exist to perform Robert’s requested personal automation. Hermes is not a public product or a shared service for other users.
