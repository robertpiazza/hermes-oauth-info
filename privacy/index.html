Google data used

Only when Robert connects a Google account and directs a task, Hermes may access or act on the following categories of Google data:

  • Gmail message content and mail metadata, including reading, searching, labeling, modifying, composing, and sending email.
  • Google Calendar event details.
  • Google Contacts profile data.
  • Google Docs document content.
  • Google Drive files and file metadata.
  • Google Sheets spreadsheets and their data.

The currently configured Google OAuth permissions are: gmail.modify, gmail.readonly, gmail.send, calendar, contacts.readonly, documents.readonly, drive.file, drive.readonly, and spreadsheets.

Google data is used only to carry out Robert’s user-directed personal automation, such as finding information, preparing or sending requested email, working with calendars, and working with files or documents. It is not used for unrelated purposes.

When a user-directed task requires interaction with a configured AI model or provider, relevant Google data or task content may be included in that interaction to perform the task. This policy does not make claims about any provider’s separate data practices.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used for data brokerage. This is the intended use of Google user data under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
